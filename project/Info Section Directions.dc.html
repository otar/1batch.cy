Help

SEPA or TBOC?
TBOC is a transfer to a Bank of Cyprus account. SEPA is a euro transfer to a bank in the SEPA zone. The page sets the type from the IBAN. To change it, open “⋯” on the row.
Which characters can I use?
A–Z, a–z, 0–9, space and / - ? : ( ) . , ' +. No accents, no Greek letters, no &. Names: 35 characters maximum. Descriptions: 140.
What does the page check?
The same rules as the command-line tool. If one check fails, the page does not make the file. The bank name comes from a short list in the page. It does not prove that the account exists.
How do I continue later?
The page keeps nothing. Download the XML or CSV file and open it next time. A past execution date moves to the next business day.
Same payments next month?
Open last month's XML file. Change the amounts and the month in the descriptions. Then download a new file.
